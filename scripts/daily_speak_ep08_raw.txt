# Episode 8 — Daily Routine & Everyday Conversations
## Daily Speak English | English Listening & Speaking Practice

**Solu:** Hello, everyone! Welcome back to Daily Speak English. I'm Solu.

**Tonu:** And I'm Tonu! Today we're practicing everyday English through a conversation about daily routines and common situations.

**Solu:** Let's start with the morning. What do you usually do after you wake up, Tonu?

**Tonu:** I usually make my bed, wash my face, drink some water, and prepare breakfast. Then I check my schedule for the day.

**Solu:** That sounds organized. I like to prepare a few things the night before, such as my clothes and work bag.

**Tonu:** That can save time in the morning. What do you usually have for breakfast?

**Solu:** It depends on the day. Sometimes I have eggs and toast; sometimes I have fruit or oatmeal. I try not to skip breakfast when I have a busy morning.

**Tonu:** Let's practice ordering food. You can say, 'Could I have a coffee and a sandwich, please?' If you need a change, say, 'Could I get that without onions?'

**Solu:** At a shop, you might ask, 'How much does this cost?' or 'Do you have this in another size?'

**Tonu:** If you're asking for directions, try, 'Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to the bus station?'

**Solu:** And if you didn't hear someone, say, 'Sorry, could you say that again?' It's polite and natural.

**Tonu:** At work or school, you might say, 'I'm running a little late,' 'I'll be there in ten minutes,' or 'Could we reschedule?'

**Solu:** These short phrases are useful because everyday communication doesn't always require complicated grammar.

**Tonu:** Exactly. The key is to practice common situations until the phrases feel natural.

**Solu:** Let's talk about evenings. After a busy day, I like to tidy my space, eat dinner, and spend a little time learning something.

**Tonu:** I like to prepare for tomorrow and then relax with a book or a podcast. A simple evening routine can help you finish the day calmly.

**Solu:** Useful expressions include 'I'm on my way,' 'That works for me,' 'No worries,' and 'I'll let you know.'

**Tonu:** Today's challenge: Describe your typical day in English, from the moment you wake up until you go to bed.

**Solu:** Thanks for practicing with us. Use English in small everyday moments, and you'll gradually become more comfortable. Goodbye!
