# Episode 7 — Career, Job Interviews & Workplace English
## Daily Speak English | English Listening & Speaking Practice

**Solu:** Welcome to Daily Speak English! I'm Solu, and today we're discussing careers, job interviews, and communicating confidently at work.

**Tonu:** I'm Tonu! Whether you're looking for your first job or hoping to grow in your career, clear communication can make a real difference.

**Solu:** Let's begin with preparation. Before an interview, learn about the organization, understand the role, and think of examples that demonstrate your skills.

**Tonu:** It's better to prepare honest examples than memorize impressive-sounding answers. Interviewers often ask how you handled a challenge or worked with a team.

**Solu:** A useful structure is to explain the situation, your responsibility, the action you took, and the result.

**Tonu:** For example: 'Our team had a tight deadline. I organized the tasks, communicated progress, and helped us deliver the project on time.'

**Solu:** When you don't know an answer, don't pretend. You can say, 'I haven't worked with that tool yet, but I'm willing to learn.'

**Tonu:** At work, clear messages save time. Include the purpose of your message, the relevant details, and the action you need.

**Solu:** Instead of writing, 'Please check,' you might write, 'Could you review the attached draft and share your feedback by Thursday?'

**Tonu:** Meetings are another place to practice. You can say, 'I'd like to add something,' 'Could you clarify that point?' or 'I agree with the main idea.'

**Solu:** If you disagree, focus on the idea rather than criticizing the person. Try, 'I see the benefit, but I have a concern about the timeline.'

**Tonu:** Professional growth also requires feedback. Ask what you can improve, then choose one or two actions to work on.

**Solu:** And don't forget workplace boundaries. Being professional doesn't mean being available every hour of the day.

**Tonu:** Useful expressions: 'I'll follow up' means you'll contact someone again. 'Let's touch base' means let's briefly discuss progress.

**Solu:** 'I'm working on it' communicates that a task is in progress. 'Could you walk me through it?' asks someone to explain a process.

**Tonu:** Today's speaking challenge: Introduce yourself in English as if you were at a job interview. Mention your experience, strengths, and one career goal.

**Solu:** Thanks for listening to Daily Speak English. Prepare carefully, communicate clearly, and keep learning. See you next time!
