# Episode 9 — Dreams, Goals & Personal Development
## Daily Speak English | English Listening & Speaking Practice

**Solu:** Welcome back to Daily Speak English! I'm Solu.

**Tonu:** And I'm Tonu! Today we're talking about dreams, goals, patience, and becoming the person you want to be.

**Solu:** Almost everyone has a dream. It might be starting a business, learning a skill, supporting family, traveling, or making a positive difference.

**Tonu:** A dream becomes easier to work toward when you turn it into a clear goal. Instead of saying, 'I want to improve,' decide what improvement looks like.

**Solu:** For example, 'I want to speak English more confidently, so I'll practice for fifteen minutes each evening.' That's specific and measurable.

**Tonu:** It's also useful to set a realistic deadline and review your progress. If the plan isn't working, adjust it rather than abandoning the goal.

**Solu:** Sometimes people feel discouraged because progress is slow. But important skills take time to develop.

**Tonu:** That's why patience matters. You may not notice improvement every day, but regular effort adds up.

**Solu:** Another challenge is fear of failure. People sometimes avoid trying because they don't want to make mistakes in front of others.

**Tonu:** But mistakes can provide information. Ask yourself what happened, what you learned, and what you can do differently next time.

**Solu:** It's also helpful to find mentors, teachers, or supportive communities. Learning from someone else's experience can save time.

**Tonu:** But don't copy another person's path exactly. Your responsibilities, resources, and interests may be different.

**Solu:** I like the phrase, 'Stay focused on your own journey.' It reminds us that comparison can distract us from our own progress.

**Tonu:** Another expression is, 'Keep your eyes on the goal.' It means remember what you're working toward, especially when things become difficult.

**Solu:** 'Learn from your mistakes' is another useful phrase. It means use setbacks as opportunities to improve.

**Tonu:** Today's question: What is one dream you hope to achieve in the next five years, and what is the first step you can take?

**Solu:** Thanks for joining us. Keep your dreams meaningful, your plans practical, and your effort steady.

**Tonu:** Believe that you can learn and improve. We'll see you in the next episode!
